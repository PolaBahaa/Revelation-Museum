================================================================================
REVELATION ROYAL MUSEUM — OPERATOR GUIDE
================================================================================

1. Copy the entire 'Revelation-Museum' folder to your Windows computer (Desktop or USB Drive).
2. Double-click 'Revelation Museum.exe'.
3. Wait a moment for the museum to open in fullscreen kiosk mode.
4. Click anywhere on the museum canvas to lock mouse pointer and begin exploration.
5. Use W / A / S / D keys to walk (Hold SHIFT to sprint).
6. Move the mouse to look around naturally.
7. Press numeric keys 1 through 6 to jump directly to specific artworks and galleries.
8. Press F or click on an artwork to enter focused Artwork Inspection Mode.
9. Press ESC to release pointer lock or exit inspection mode.
10. To close the museum, press Alt + F4.
